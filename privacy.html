Global TV EPG 私隱政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 14 日

我哋唔收集任何資料

Global TV EPG 唔會收集、儲存或分享任何個人資料。App 冇用戶帳號、冇分析追蹤、冇廣告 SDK、冇第三方數據收集。

網絡使用

App 會連接公開 EPG 及台徽來源下載節目資料和圖片，亦會連接 Apple 服務以處理訂閱及 iCloud 同步。我哋冇設立分析追蹤或用戶資料伺服器。

本機儲存及 iCloud

節目表快取及已下載台徽儲存喺你部裝置。最愛、想睇清單及原有偏好可透過 Apple iCloud Key-Value Store 喺你自己嘅裝置之間同步。

Pro 訂閱用戶亦可透過 CloudKit 將各地區嘅頻道排序及隱藏設定儲存至自己嘅私人 iCloud 資料庫，並喺登入同一 iCloud 帳戶、具有有效 Pro 權限嘅裝置上同步。此功能預設開啟，你可隨時喺 App 設定或「自訂頻道」關閉。關閉後會停止 CloudKit 同步，本機設定及已儲存嘅 iCloud 資料會保留；離線修改會先存喺本機，恢復連線後再合併。

CloudKit 同步唔會包含付款資料、訂閱收據、Apple 帳戶憑證或節目表／台徽快取。我哋唔會將呢啲私人設定傳送至自己嘅伺服器，亦唔會用作廣告或追蹤。iCloud 服務由 Apple 提供及管理，適用 Apple 嘅私隱政策及條款。

第三方資料來源

節目資料由第三方公開 EPG 來源提供，其內容、準確性及可用性非本 App 控制。節目資料及相關知識產權屬各自的電視台或營運商所有。詳見使用條款及免責聲明。

兒童

本 App 不針對兒童，亦不會有意收集任何人（包括 13 歲以下人士）嘅個人資料。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 14 November 2026

We collect no data. Global TV EPG does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party data collection.

Network usage. The app downloads programme listings and channel logos from public sources and connects to Apple services for subscriptions and iCloud sync. We operate no analytics or user-data server.

Local storage and iCloud. Programme caches and downloaded channel logos remain on your device. Favourites, the watchlist and existing preferences may sync between your devices through Apple’s iCloud Key-Value Store.

Pro subscribers can also store channel order and hidden-channel settings for each region in their private CloudKit database. These settings sync to devices using the same iCloud account with an active Pro entitlement. CloudKit sync is on by default and can be turned off in Settings or Customise channels. Turning it off stops sync and keeps local settings and previously saved iCloud data. Offline edits are saved locally and merged when connectivity returns.

The CloudKit payload does not contain payment details, subscription receipts, Apple account credentials, programme caches or logo files. We do not send these private settings to our own servers or use them for advertising or tracking. Apple provides and manages iCloud under its own privacy policy and terms.
